02Our story

It started with
Kim’s hands.

Kim has trained as a gymnast for many years. Her hands cracked and tore open again and again. Normal creams were too greasy for the bar or made her calluses soft.

So four athletes set out to build the care we always wanted: for the hands, and for the head behind them.

Why we exist

What gymnasts face,
and who listens.

Gymnasts ask everything of their hands and their heads. Most products and most companies were never built around them. We were.

01

Dry, cracked, torn hands.

Chalk and bars tear skin open. Normal creams are too greasy for the bar or soften the calluses gymnasts need.

HOLD is a water-based, grip-neutral gel-cream, designed to absorb before the next rotation.

02

“Tape it and go again.”

Training through pain is still treated as normal.

We say the opposite: open rips need cleaning, covering and rest. It is printed on our pack, and we will never advertise pain as strength.

03

Pressure nobody talks about.

Perfection is the standard, and the mind rarely gets a coach.

Every tube carries free, independent help and a 1-minute breathing reset.

04

Brands that never ask.

Sports care is built for climbers, CrossFit or kitchens. No brand is built with gymnastics clubs.

Athletes help steer ours, and they hold a veto over our purpose.

03The Golden Share

Our purpose
can’t be sold.

Promises are easy to make and easy to drop, especially after a company is sold. So we built ours into the company itself. An independent athletes’ trust holds one special share in HOLD: the Golden Share. It earns no money. It has one job: to keep us committed to our core values.

Holds the Golden Share
HOLD Athletes Trust e.V.

Independent association. Board: an elected athlete, a safeguarding expert and a sports psychologist. Founders and investors are excluded.

VETONO DIVIDEND
The company
HOLD GmbH

Founders and investors own and run the business. Every decision about our purpose needs the trust’s consent.

Without the athletes’ consent, nobody can:

1

Change our purpose

Caring for gymnasts’ hands and minds stays written into our articles.

2

Cut the HOLD Fund formula

2% of revenue from the first euro, at least €5k a year, plus 10% of profit.

3

Sell it away

A merger or sale that would drop our purpose or the fund can be blocked.

Why this matters to you: when you buy HOLD, you are not buying a promise that lasts until the next owner. The people we serve have the final word on what we stand for.

Every club season agreement includes an independent workshop on pressure, mistakes and speaking up. Run by a sports psychologist, after signing, with no product in the room.

Charitable tubes

For every ten tubes a club orders, one goes free to an athlete who needs it, passed on discreetly by the club’s youth officer.

How we advertise
18+

No targeted ads to anyone under 18. We show hands, never bodies. We make care claims, never cure claims. And we never sell pain as strength.

We keep three separate columns and never add them up: the HOLD Fund is our commitment; club workshops are part of our club package and booked as marketing; charitable tubes are product access.

How we measure it

Healthier hands. Lighter heads.

Good intentions are not impact. We will publish a yearly impact report, checked by an independent evaluator, and publish it even when the results are not what we hoped.
