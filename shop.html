How to use: File · Care · Reset

File hard callus edges gently. Care: a pea-sized amount after training or before bed. Reset: scan the code on the tube and breathe for a minute.

For intact skin only

HOLD is a cosmetic for stressed, intact skin. Do not apply to open rips: clean and cover them, rest, and use HOLD once the skin has closed. Our rip-care guide will be reviewed by a sports physician before launch.

What’s inside

A light, water-based gel-cream with about 5% urea to hold moisture without softening calluses. No mineral oil, no heavy wax. Natural cosmetics certification (NATRUE) planned. Dermatologically tested before launch.

Pack & production

Formula developed in-house with our gymnast panel, produced by a certified contract manufacturer in Germany. Tube made from 50% recycled PE, box from FSC paper.
