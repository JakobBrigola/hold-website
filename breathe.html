Before you start

Gentle. Honest. Private.

01Breathe gently, never force.

Comfortable is the right amount. If a count feels too long, make it shorter.

02Dizzy? Stop.

If you feel dizzy or light-headed, stop and breathe normally.

03Care, not cure.

These exercises can help you wind down and refocus. They are not therapy or medical treatment.

04Nothing is stored or sent.

You don’t need to buy anything. No login, no cookies, no tracking. Sound and vibration cues are optional and off by default.
